Vision & Brainstorming: The "Why"

LockedIn

The vision for LockedIn started with a simple need: automating basketball shot logging, analytics, and highlight clipping without relying on expensive, cloud-dependent infrastructure. The goal was to engineer a local, edge-AI hardware and software solution that allows players to focus entirely on athletic movement and shooting mechanics. These early persona, analytics, and biometrics blueprints laid the foundation for the real-time vision tracking system.

Match Play

Match Play was conceptualized to bridge the gap between a traditional golf scorecard and a modern social platform. The core motivation was to build a robust, scalable architecture—from the Python REST API backend to the interactive frontend—that handles round tracking, algorithmic feeds, and player profiles seamlessly. These early agile planning sprints and architecture diagrams ensured the database and routing logic were mapped before writing the first line of code.
